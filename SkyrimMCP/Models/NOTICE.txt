face_detector.onnx and face_landmarks.onnx are Google's MediaPipe Face Landmarker models
(face_landmarker.task, float16, version 1: face_detector.tflite and face_landmarks_detector.tflite),
converted from TensorFlow Lite to ONNX with tf2onnx by scripts/face-model-convert.sh. No weights were changed.

Source: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task
Model card: https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/face_landmarker

pose_landmarks_detector.tflite), converted the same way. Source:
