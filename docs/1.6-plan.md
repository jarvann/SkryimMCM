# SkyLink AI 1.6: Characters

The loop 1.6 closes: a description or photo becomes a face, the face becomes an NPC, the NPC becomes a follower, and the
follower becomes a mod the player could share (FEAT-284 is the acceptance test). Tracker: phase 18.

## What RaceMenu already gives us (found 2026-11-21)

RaceMenu's `CharGen` script (in `RaceMenu.bsa`, `scripts\source\chargen.psc`, script version 4) has the pieces the riskiest
items need, so SkyLink calls them through the Papyrus bridge instead of writing them first:

| CharGen function | What it does | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `LoadCharacter(Actor, Race, name)` | Loads a `.jslot` preset onto any actor, NPCs included, and RaceMenu keeps the mapping | FEAT-122 (a spawned NPC's face) |
| `ExportHead(fileName)` | Writes the **player's** head mesh (NIF) and tint mask (DDS) to `SKSE\Plugins\CharGen\` | FEAT-281, FEAT-283 (FaceGen files for a follower mod) |
| `SaveExternalCharacter` / `LoadExternalCharacter` | Preset plus NIF and DDS as one "external" character | FEAT-283 fallback |
| `DeleteFaceGenData(ActorBase)` | Removes an NPC's FaceGeom NIF and FaceTint DDS | FEAT-256 (rebuild a changed NPC) |

So with RaceMenu installed:
- **Face to NPC:** write the blueprint's face as a preset (the face builder already does), `LoadCharacter` it onto the spawned NPC.
- **NPC to FaceGen files:** put the face on the player for a moment (the face builder's own path, with the player's face
  saved first), `ExportHead`, copy the NIF and DDS to `FaceGenData\FaceGeom\<plugin>\<formid>.nif` and the textures folder,
  restore the player's face. The game then shows the NPC's face without RaceMenu at runtime, which is what a shared
  follower mod needs.

Without RaceMenu, FEAT-281's own NIF writer (head parts assembled with their morphs, written clean room from public format
notes, never nifly) is the fallback. It moves after the RaceMenu path works end to end.

## Open questions

- Can `LoadCharacter` on a runtime-created NPC base survive a save and load once SkyLink recreates the base from its
  co-save? RaceMenu maps presets by form ID, and a recreated runtime form may get a new one. Test in round A.
- `ExportHead` exports the player only: confirm putting a face on the player and restoring it is lossless (the face
  builder's ResetFace and preset save cover most of it).
- Voice types with follower lines: the vanilla list is known; modded voice types need a check of their dialogue.

## Stretch: voice, dialogue and live behaviour (agreed 2026-11-21)

Skyrim reads new plugins only at startup, so SkyLink ships one small carrier plugin (installed once) and the creative loop
runs live through it; only the finished follower mod needs a restart.

| Item | Live in game | Needs a restart |
|---|---|---|
| FEAT-293 voice lines (ElevenLabs, player's key) | Generate, hear on the PC, convert (Creation Kit's xWMAEncode and LipGen, .fuz), NPC says it lip-synced through a carrier line | Installing the carrier plugin, once |
| FEAT-294 custom dialogue | Lines auditioned through carrier topics | The exported follower mod |
| FEAT-295 behaviour beyond the defaults | Package overrides, carrier packages with places and targets filled at runtime | New package logic in the exported mod |

Order: after FEAT-122 (a spawned NPC to speak). Batch F, voice first.

## Decisions (2026-11-21)

- **The goal of the loop:** the player exports a character into **their own ESP/ESL** and curates it as a dedicated mod
  (FEAT-283). Everything before that is preview.
- **NPC slots:** `SkyLinkAI.esl` (NPC slots plus FEAT-293's carrier topics) ships as an **optional FOMOD file, on by
  default** (INFRA-025). Without it, SpawnNpc gives a session-only preview from a runtime form.
- **Dependencies (FEAT-296):** every form a character uses is recorded with its plugin while it's built, so the export
  lists the Nexus mods to select as requirements (from MO2's meta.ini or Vortex's records), with why each is needed.
