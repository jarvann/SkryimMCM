# SkyLink AI 1.6 "Characters": full test

You are testing SkyLink AI 1.6 in the running game, through SkyLink's tools. Test every feature below until each one either
works as described or has a bug report. How you test each one is up to you.

## Starting point

- A new game: a new **female** adventurer, made by Cory in character creation. Start after she has control of her character
  and is out of the opening, then save as `SkyLink_16_Start`.
- Before starting, Claude Desktop must have been quit from the tray and reopened, so the 1.6 tools are loaded (200 tools).
- SkyLinkAI.esp (the optional NPC slot plugin) is in the load order. Other saves on this game already hold SkyLink
  characters: their slots are shared by every save and must not be touched.

## Rules

- Ask Cory, and wait for his answer, for every choice that belongs to the player: follower, marriage, attraction, home, the
  family story, clearing land, finalizing a character, answering a message box. Never pick an answer for him.
- Save only under new names starting `SkyLink_16_`. Never overwrite or delete a save that already exists.
- Tell Cory before loading a save, restarting or quitting the game, and wait for his go if he is in the middle of something.
- Report each bug as you find it: the call, its exact reply, what you saw in game, and what you expected.

## Features to test

### 1. The game and its folders
- GetGameEnvironment names the right store (Steam or GOG), versions, game, Data, documents and saves folders, INIs in use,
  mod manager, and (under MO2) the instance and profile.

### 2. Saves
- Saving under a new name works and the save appears.
- Loading a save of a *different* character is refused at first, with the reason; it loads only when asked again
  explicitly. Loading one of her own saves isn't refused.

### 3. Message boxes
- ReadMessageBox reports the box on screen: its text, its buttons and an ID.
- AnswerMessageBox presses the button Cory chooses, and is refused when another box has replaced it.
- `always` remembers a single-button box, and SkyLink closes that box by itself from then on.

### 4. Making characters (SpawnNpc)
- Before spawning anyone, SkyLink asks whether they follow, whether they can marry, who they're attracted to (only when a
  mod that keeps attraction is installed) and where they live. Nothing is spawned until those are answered.
- A dry run says what would be made.
- Race works by the name the game shows (one-word and two-word names), by editor ID and by form ID. An unknown race is
  refused with a clear message.
- Women and men, of several races. Each face option works: the template's face, and the player's own face (with her outfit
  and body preset).
- Voice, outfit, essential and keep-schedule each do what they say.
- A spawned character takes an NPC slot and is lasting. Her face is right at once (not dark, grey or purple). It is still
  right after a save and reload, and after a full game restart. Hair colour, voice and body are kept too.
- With no free slot of that race and sex, spawning is refused. A preview character is made only when asked for, with a
  warning about saving.
- Hair with physics (SMP), if such hair is installed, moves on a spawned character and doesn't fall to the floor.

### 5. Followers, marriage, attraction
- A character made a follower can be recruited with the game's own dialogue, follows, and can be dismissed. Turning
  follower off works. A voice without follower lines is replaced with one that has them.
- A marryable character can be proposed to with the Amulet of Mara and wed. A step-sibling can marry; a blood relative
  can't be made marryable.
- SetAttraction lists the attraction mods installed, and writes the player's answer into each of them.

### 6. Player Family
- The list of family roles. Giving a role, changing it, and removing it (`none`). Relatives can be of any race.
- A relative made with a family role takes after the player in face and hair colour, by the resemblance asked for.
- Marriage is allowed only in roles that aren't kin by blood.

### 7. The family story
- FamilyStory asks one question at a time and keeps the answers for this character only (not for other characters' saves).
- An answer outside the allowed choices is refused.
- When the story is told, it suggests the region of Skyrim and the style of home that fit, the homes usable now, and the
  relatives to make. Characters made from it are listed as made.
- Reset forgets the story.

### 8. Homes
- SetHome lists the choices. The choices offer only the player's houses (one entry per house, none she doesn't own), the
  family home, and SkyLink homes that exist in this game (not those from other saves). Same-named homes are told apart.
- The resident lives at the home they're given: `here`, a house she owns (buy one in the game), the family home, or a
  SkyLink home.
- The first relative given a home makes it the family home. SetFamilyHome adds a home, sets the main one, and removes one.
- After a wedding, SetSpouseHome moves the spouse to a house she owns, and to a SkyLink home. The game's "visit your
  spouse's house" marker (map and compass) follows, and the spouse's home is recorded.

### 9. Home layouts
- BuildHomeLayout lists the layouts, does a dry run, and builds the homestead:
  - in front of her, on uneven ground and on a slope;
  - at its original spot;
  - in place of an existing home.
- A built home stands right:
  - the door at ground level, and every yard piece on the ground;
  - the bed inside the tent;
  - the cooking pot hanging from its spit over the fire;
  - nothing built on top of the player.
- The site survey moves or turns the home away from rocks, trees and steep ground, and says what it found.
- Clearing the land (asked first) switches off the rocks, trees and clutter in the way but never the cliffs. It reports
  seams, and the land under the home has no holes or bad terrain mismatches.
- A resident given at build time lives there.
- SaveHomeLayout saves only the home's own pieces, never the game's objects near it. The saved layout builds again.
- RemoveHomeLayout:
  - the dry run lists exactly the home's pieces;
  - removing takes them all away;
  - it names the residents, and the home stops being the family home;
  - what was cleared comes back.
- Homes and their residents survive a save and reload.

### 10. What a character needs, and exporting it
- CharacterRequirements lists, for a spawned character, the required mods (race, head parts, hair, voice, skin, face file
  textures, hair physics) and the optional ones (what she wears or carries). Each has its mod and Nexus page where known. A
  worn item that has no model for the character's race is flagged.
- ExportCharacter:
  - asks follower and marriage (and, for a relative, whether to keep the family role) before writing anything;
  - does a dry run;
  - writes a zip to SkyLink's Build folder, never into Data;
  - offers sensible home choices (no invisible markers or ore veins first);
  - leaves out worn items with no model for the race, and says so;
  - the README lists the requirements.
- A light plugin with no SkyLink master, unless the family role is kept.
- Installed with the mod manager, with the spawned one removed: the exported character appears at her place in a save.
  She has the same face, hair colour, voice and outfit, and can be recruited or married as chosen.

### 11. Finalizing a character
- FinalizeCharacter without a choice says, for this character, what the live and the standalone routes each need, keep and
  lose. That includes:
  - for a spouse, that the marriage stays with the live one;
  - for a SkyLink-built home, that it exists in this save only;
  - body morphs.
- Live: the choice is recorded, and anything still undecided (no home) is named.
- Standalone: the choice is recorded, and the export, install and removal steps are given.
- Refused for a preview, and for a character whose face file isn't in place.
- ListNpcSlots shows how each character was finalized.

### 12. NPC slots
- ListNpcSlots lists every slot taken in this game, including characters from other saves, with the face files in place.
- RemoveSpawnedNpc removes a spawned character (never one of the game's own) and frees its slot. A slot freed this session
  isn't given out again until the game restarts.

### 13. Scripts in archives
- GetScriptSource finds a script source that is packed in a BSA archive.

### 14. Throughout
- No call freezes the game ("not responding") or crashes it. A save holding spawned characters and homes loads.
- Script calls on spawned characters answer at once. If any character's script calls all wait, report it (BUG-118: seen
  once, cause unknown).

## Finishing

- Remove every character, home and change made by this test:
  - remove the characters and homes;
  - give back any cleared land;
  - remove the exported mod from the mod manager.

  ListNpcSlots must show only the characters that were there before.
- Save the final state as `SkyLink_16_End`.
- Report a table with one row per feature above: passed, failed (with the bug) or not tested (with why). Then list the bugs.
