File: `SkyLinkAI-v1.6.0.zip` (copied to `C:\Users\cory\Downloads`). On the Files tab, "Update" the existing 1.5.0 file row,

## File description (255 characters max)

Characters: the AI makes lasting relatives, followers and people to marry, tells your family's story, builds them homes and
exports a character as their own mod. Optional NPC slots plugin in the installer. About 200 tools.

## Changelog (one entry per line)

SpawnNpc: lasting characters with your face or a template's; follower, marriage, attraction and home asked first
NPC slots (optional SkyLinkAI.esp, light): made characters are kept in saves; faces right at once, MO2 included
Followers recruited with the game's own dialogue; marriage with the Amulet of Mara; attraction mods supported
Player Family roles (any race, resemblance to the player); step-siblings can marry, blood relatives can't
FamilyStory: the family's history, one question at a time, with the region, home and relatives to make
Homes: SetHome, family homes, the spouse's home (marker follows); homestead layouts built, saved and removed
CharacterRequirements, ExportCharacter (a character as a standalone mod) and FinalizeCharacter
ListFaceSliders: the RaceMenu sliders an install has; face edits work under MO2
Fix: a female character made male first kept male makeup layers (pale lower lip); RepairFace puts them right
SkyLink's saves are listed under the character in the Load menu; race-menu ID changes keep saves together (LinkCharacterIds)
ReadMessageBox and AnswerMessageBox; GetGameEnvironment
Updating from 1.5: restart Skyrim; quit Claude Desktop from the tray and reopen it for the new tools

## Sticky post (BBCode)

[b]SkyLink AI 1.6.0 "Characters" is out.[/b]

The AI can now make characters that stay in your game: relatives, followers and people to marry, with your own face or a
template's, living where you choose. It can walk you through your family's story, build them a homestead, and turn a character
into a mod of their own. The installer offers the optional NPC slots plugin (SkyLinkAI.esp, a light plugin, on by default):
keep it if you want made characters kept in saves.

Updating from 1.5: the plugin DLL changed, so restart Skyrim. New tools appear after Claude Desktop is quit from the system
tray and opened again.
